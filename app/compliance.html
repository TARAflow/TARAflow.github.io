App / 09

Compliance

The requirement matrix of your component — IEC 62443-4-2 in the classic edition (2019) or the CRA edition EN IEC 62443-4-2 — derived from the DFD, the risk treatment and the project data you already have. Rows are checked, not ticked.

01

Choosing the Matrix

  • The tag decides: IEC 62443 for the classic edition with its device categories, EN IEC 62443 (CRA) for the CRA edition (draft prAA:2026, marked as draft)
  • With both tags the two editions appear side by side as sub-tabs
  • One statement per requirement holds in both editions — switching between the classic and the CRA tag keeps your statements
02

Security Levels

SL-T

Target

Choose the component (or the whole model) and set the target security level per foundational requirement.

SL-C

Achieved

What the product reaches from the model and the mitigations marked done.

SL-C planned

Planned

What it reaches once the chosen mitigations are done.

With system

With measures of the system

The value including the supporting measures of the system around the component.

03

Derived, Not Ticked

  • Every row is checked against the DFD properties, the mitigations of the risk treatment (per element; only done ones count for the achieved value) and supporting measures
  • An unset property is unknown, never not applicable
  • What the model cannot show gets a statement with reasoning and evidence; TARAflow tells you when the model changed since
  • Hover a requirement number for its title and summary, click it for everything — how TARAflow checks it, the elements concerned, fitting catalog mitigations, your statement. The summaries are in our own words; the standard is authoritative
04

What to Do Next

  • A to-do list up to the SL-T names, per element, the property to set or check, marks what the planned mitigations close and lists the missing statements
  • A click on the element shows it in the diagram; a filter shows the rows by status
  • A quick guide walks through the steps
  • The report gets a chapter with the security levels, the statement of applicability (with the source of every row), the CRA view by Annex I letter and the gaps with suggested mitigations — in every format and the CLI

Example: the Pump Station Controller in TARAflow_Examples is an IEC 62443 / CRA example project to try the matrix on.
