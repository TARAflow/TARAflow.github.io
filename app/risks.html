Risk Assessment

Score likelihood per threat, compute Risk = Impact × Likelihood, prioritize mitigations with MoSCoW, and compare risk before and after controls.

01

Risk Scoring

  • Impact comes from the security goals the threat violates on its linked assets, including per-goal impact
  • Likelihood is scored using OWASP Risk Rating factors (skill level, motive, opportunity, size, ease of discovery and exploit, awareness, intrusion detection), ISO/IEC 18045 attack potential (ISO/SAE 21434) or ETSI TVRA — each level named by the situation it stands for
  • Risk = Impact × Likelihood on a configurable N×N matrix
  • Risk levels: Low / Medium / High / Critical — threshold configurable per project
  • ISO/SAE 21434: the risk value 1–5 (clause 15.8) from the matrix of Annex H.8 replaces I × L — impact on the 4-level scale Negligible / Moderate / Major / Severe × attack feasibility, shown with a word (Minimal / Low / Medium / High / Critical); the heat map is the 4 × 4 matrix of H.8
02

Impact Aggregation

  • Harm floor (standard, EN 50742): safety, physical damage and environment are never diluted — the impact is the highest of them or the weighted mean of the other factors
  • Maximum (ISO/SAE 21434, ETSI TVRA): the worst impact category, unweighted
  • Impact weights default to the asset criterion weights and can be overridden per factor
  • Switching the aggregation shows every risk that changes before it is applied; the risk dialog and the report explain how each impact was formed
03

Prioritization

  • MoSCoW: Must fix / Should fix / Could fix / Won't fix
  • ISO/SAE 21434: the attack paths of one tree form a threat scenario — its value and residual are the highest of its paths; treatment options Avoid / Reduce / Share / Retain (15.9); a review list names Retain at a value of 4 or more and Reduce at value 1 — nothing changes automatically
  • Before/after toggle: compare risk score before and after proposed mitigations
  • Won't fix decisions require a cost-benefit justification (Phase 5 governance)
  • Risk register exportable to documentation and ticket systems
04

Leveled DFDs

  • Risks are grouped per decomposed multiprocess, like the threats
  • One risk matrix per DFD group instead of a single roll-up
  • Filter by page and analysis state
