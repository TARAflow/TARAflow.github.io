App / 04

Assets

Assets are the items of value in your system — data, functions, infrastructure, and physical components. TARAflow derives CIANAAA security goals from the DFD relations and the asset impact; the analyst confirms, adjusts or excludes them. The goals drive threat generation and the risk impact.

01

Asset Groups

Data

Data Assets

Configurations, firmware images, cryptographic keys, process parameters, user credentials.

Function

Function Assets

Control logic, safety functions, service availability, processing capability.

System

System Assets

Embedded controller, gateway, server — the platform as a whole.

Physical

Physical Assets

PCB, enclosure, cable routing — subject to physical attack vectors.

Service

Service Assets

Cloud services, APIs, communication infrastructure.

Process

Process Assets

Business processes, operational procedures, maintenance workflows.

02

CIANAAA Security Goals

  • Confidentiality (C) → Information Disclosure (I)
  • Integrity (I) → Tampering (T)
  • Availability (A) → Denial of Service (D)
  • Non-repudiation (N) → Repudiation (R)
  • Authentication (AuthN) → Spoofing (S)
  • Authorization (AuthZ) → Elevation of Privilege (E)
  • Accountability (Acc) → Repudiation (R)
  • A STRIDE category is generated only if it is technically possible for the element and violates an active goal (level ≠ none)
03

Derived Goals, Analyst Decisions

  • Goals are suggested from the asset's DFD relations (e.g. data transported or stored); the level follows the asset impact — every card says why this goal and why this level
  • The analyst adjusts a level, adds a goal or excludes one — always with a rationale; an exclusion stays visible in the table and the report
  • The suggestion a decision was made against is recorded; when its basis changes later, the goal is flagged for review
  • An unassessed goal never shows as "Low": it says "assessment required" and points to the impact rating
  • Impact per goal: a goal can rate a criterion lower than the asset (e.g. safety for integrity) — never higher
04

Review and Cross-Checks

  • Goal chips in the asset table: level and state at a glance, decisions marked
  • Findings panel below the table opens the asset where a finding is fixed; "Needs review only" filter
  • Threat ↔ goal cross-checks: threats that violate no active goal, goals no threat violates, threats per goal
  • Report: security-goal table with level, state, basis and damage-scenario consequence
