Threat Analysis & Risk Assessment

A knowledge-graph-driven TARA workflow for connected and embedded systems.

TARAflow builds a knowledge graph from your DFD, its assets and the relationships between them. Hazard items can sit inside the same relationship chains, giving you a traceable cause-and-effect path from a hazard, through threats and risks, to mitigation.

Core idea

Three principles the tool is built around

Knowledge graph

DFD, assets and their relationships

The DFD and its assets aren't modeled as flat lists. Asset-to-asset and asset-to-DFD relationships are captured explicitly, forming a knowledge graph that the rest of the analysis is derived from.

Cause & effect

Safety annotations on relationships

Relationships can carry safety information directly — Impact (reversible / irreversible / fatal) and Relevance (direct / indirect) — so hazard potential per ISO 12100 comes from the same graph, not a second document.

One fixed workflow

General → DFD → Assets → Threats → Risks

The process is always the same: General → Hazards → DFD → Assets → Threats → optional Attack Trees → Risks → Documentation. Without safety enabled, the Hazards step is simply hidden.

Capabilities

What's in the current build

01

Knowledge graph

Asset-to-asset and asset-to-DFD relationships form a knowledge graph — and can include hazard items for full cause-and-effect chains.

02

Embedded system modeling

Interface, Physical Boundary and Chip Boundary elements make physical attack surfaces — housings, ports, debug headers — explicit instead of invisible.

03

DFD-driven threat generation

STRIDE threats proposed per element and per interaction, using context from the modeled system.

04

Closed-loop mitigation

Jira-tracked treatment with before/after risk comparison, synced back to the model.

05

Attack-path analysis

Threat-anchored attack trees with feasibility and attack-potential evaluation.

06

Security & safety together

A BowTie-based hazard workflow feeds physical-impact and safety-override rules into the same risk model.

07

Auditability

Git-based history, signed commits, authorized-signer management and the Audit Verification Engine.

08

Headless reporting

The same Markdown, AsciiDoc, HTML, PDF and StrictDoc output from taraflow-report in CI/CD, no desktop app required.
