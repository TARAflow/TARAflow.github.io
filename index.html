01

What is TARAflow?

TARA — Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment — is a cornerstone of modern product security. TARAflow makes it graph-based and traceable: every threat links back to the asset it endangers and forward to the control that mitigates it.

Starting from a data-flow diagram, the tool derives protection goals, generates STRIDE-based threats per element and per interaction — plus attack-path threats from attack trees and hazard-driven scenarios from a Bowtie analysis — scores risk as Impact × Likelihood, and tracks each mitigation through to verification.

The result is a living assessment — not a static spreadsheet — that holds up in audits and evolves with your system.

02

Capabilities

Graph-based modeling

Assets, data flows and threats live in a connected graph — relationships are first-class, not buried in rows.

DFD-driven threats

Import or draw a data-flow diagram; TARAflow proposes STRIDE threats per element and per interaction automatically.

Quantified risk

Impact × likelihood scoring with configurable factor sets (OWASP, ETSI, EN 50742) and threshold-based risk levels.

Attack-tree modeling

Model attacker paths as trees; feasibility per ISO 21434 Annex G or IEC 62443, synced into the risk register with full provenance.

Safety & security

A dedicated Hazard Identification (Bowtie) workflow feeds physical impact and safety-override rules directly into the risk model.

Closed-loop mitigations

Implemented controls are auto-detected from DFD properties and pre-flagged. Nothing is suppressed — the before/after delta is always visible.

Audit-ready output

Generate documentation that maps cleanly to IEC 62443, ISO 21434 and CRA requirements — defensible by design.

Headless reporting

Generate the same Markdown, AsciiDoc, HTML, PDF and StrictDoc output from the command line — built for CI/CD pipelines, no desktop app required.
