What is TARAflow?

TARA — Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment — is a cornerstone of modern product security. TARAflow makes it graph-based and traceable: every threat links back to the asset it endangers and forward to the control that mitigates it.

Starting from a data-flow diagram, the tool derives protection goals, generates STRIDE-based threats per element and interaction, scores risk as impact × likelihood, and tracks each mitigation through to verification.

The result is a living assessment — not a static spreadsheet — that holds up in audits and evolves with your system.

02

Capabilities

Graph-based modeling

Assets, data flows and threats live in a connected graph — relationships are first-class, not buried in rows.

DFD-driven threats

Import or draw a data-flow diagram; TARAflow proposes STRIDE threats per element and per interaction automatically.

Quantified risk

Impact × likelihood scoring with configurable factors (OWASP, ETSI, EN 50742) and threshold-based risk levels.

Safety & security

Native safety-security integration — physical impact and safety overrides feed directly into the risk model.

Mitigation tracking

Link controls to threats, track implementation progress, and tie each mitigation to a verification.

Audit-ready output

Generate documentation that maps cleanly to IEC 62443, ISO 21434 and CRA requirements — defensible by design.
