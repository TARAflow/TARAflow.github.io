Risk Engine

Risk scoring, factor model, MoSCoW treatment, and before/after impact comparison.

01

Risk Model

  • Risk = Impact × Likelihood on a configurable N×N severity matrix
  • Impact: derived from asset CIANAAA levels + safety override
  • Likelihood: OWASP Risk Rating factors (6 threat-agent + 6 vulnerability factors)
  • reconcileFactorRatings(): re-computes aggregate likelihood when individual factors change
  • Safety Override: direct relevance + fatal impact → CRITICAL, independent of numeric score
02

Risk Treatment

  • MitigationStatus: proposed | accepted | implemented | verified
  • ImplementationProgress: not_started | in_progress | complete
  • RiskMitigationStatusDialog: separate concern from MitigationStatus
  • Before/after: riskBefore stored at assessment time, riskAfter re-computed after mitigations
  • Won't-fix: requires CBA justification stored in audit trail
